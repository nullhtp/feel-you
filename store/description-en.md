## Full Description

Feel You is an accessibility app that teaches Morse code through vibration and touch — no sight or hearing needed.

Designed for deaf-blind individuals, Feel You turns your phone into a patient, personal Morse code teacher. The app vibrates each character's Morse pattern and waits for you to tap it back. Take as long as you need — there are no time limits, no quizzes, and no pressure.

**How it works:**
- The app vibrates a Morse code pattern for a character
- Tap it back: the left half of the screen is a dot, the right half is a dash
- Get instant vibration feedback: five quick taps for correct, one long buzz for incorrect
- The pattern keeps repeating patiently; you decide when to move on
- Move at your own pace through digits, letters, and words

**Features:**
- Full Morse code curriculum: digits (0-9), plus the letters and 20 everyday words of English, Arabic, Russian, Spanish, and French
- Vibration-only feedback — works without seeing or hearing the screen
- Intuitive gesture navigation: swipe left or right to move between characters, up or down to change level, shake to go home, double shake to change language
- Language picker that works by touch and vibration alone, and remembers your choice
- Landscape orientation for comfortable two-handed use
- Companion overlay for sighted helpers to follow along
- No ads, no in-app purchases, no data collection

**Supported languages:**
- English (International Morse Code)
- Arabic (Arabic Morse Code)
- Russian (Russian Morse Code)
- Spanish (International Morse Code with Ñ)
- French (International Morse Code with À, Ç, É, È)

Feel You is more than a learning tool — it's the first step toward independent communication for deaf-blind people through a device they already carry.
